Who we are

BridgePoint Global Consulting, Inc. is a California-based international consulting firm. We help governments, businesses, investors, and institutions build strategic partnerships and reach markets they could not open alone.

Our work is advisory and practical. We identify the right partners, plan the entry, and stay with the engagement through execution — across public and private sectors, in the places where the two have to meet.

Every engagement is built on the same commitment: ethical counsel, clear judgment, and outcomes a client can point to long after the work is done.

Mission

To connect organizations across borders through strategic consulting, trusted relationships, and innovative business solutions that promote sustainable economic growth and international cooperation.

Vision

To become one of the world's leading international consulting firms, recognized for integrity, professionalism, and excellence in global business development and strategic advisory services.

How we work

Five stages. The middle one carries the weight.

Introductions are where cross-border work is made or lost, so that is where we put the most care — and where the rest of the structure hangs from.

Stage 01

Assess

Understand the client's objectives, constraints, and the real conditions of the market.

Stage 02

Plan

Develop a customized strategy built on market realities rather than assumptions.

Stage 03

Connect

Facilitate introductions and strategic relationships where they are appropriate and useful.

Stage 04

Execute

Support implementation with ongoing advisory and hands-on project management.

Stage 05

Deliver

Measure outcomes and refine the strategy so the result holds over the long term.

Engagements can begin at any stage. Most clients come to us at Plan or Connect.
